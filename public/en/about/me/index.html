I Have My Own Website! (Finally!)

March 15, 2026 | Author: Sera

I’ve always thought it was incredibly cool to have a website with your own name followed by github.io. I started trying to build one in my first year of university. Back then I didn’t have much to show, and I kept imagining myself putting in all that work only for nobody to visit. I learned the basics of Hugo and promptly gave up.

So what finally got me to finish it? I’m looking for a summer internship, and I wanted my portfolio to give a fuller picture of me and my work. (Whether a page full of bad jokes helps with that is another question…)

Anyway, the plan was to grab a template and vibe-code my way through it in an afternoon. My wish list turned out to be a bit odd, and I ended up building almost the entire front end by hand. I wrote up the process on the blog if you’re curious.

P.S. I actually had a lot of fun writing the front end. If AI weren’t already this good at it, I might seriously have gone into front-end development. Ah well. That one stings a little.

Back to the site. I wanted three things: a clear layout, something I could maintain myself, and room for people to find what they came for.

The first two are pretty self-explanatory. The third took me a little longer to pin down. Some visitors want to see what I can make. Others might want to get to know the person who made it. I wanted both kinds of visit to feel at home here.

If you’re here for the work, you can browse Projects and Blog straight from the homepage. That’s where I keep what I’ve made and what I’ve been experimenting with.

If you’re even a little curious about me, head over to About. From time to time I’ll post projects that went spectacularly wrong, things I’ve learned, and whatever else ends up here.

That’s it. Thanks for stopping by, and have fun!
